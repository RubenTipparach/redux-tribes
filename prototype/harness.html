Help
Drag inside the dashed ring to place the destination. The ring is the grabbable part of the working altitude, which is also the plane the envelope is sliced at, so the two are one idea rather than a hidden number. Drag anywhere outside it and the camera moves instead, so looking around never costs you an order you did not mean to give.
Q/E, or ▼/▲ in the row under the map, raise and lower that plane by 5. Watch the slice shrink as you climb away from the hull: that is the spherical slice, and it is what the altitude costs you in lateral room.
Drag the pip on the ring at the destination, or shift-drag, to command a heading. The solid arrow is the heading the hull can actually reach in one turn; if you ask for more than yaw/pitch allows, a dashed arrow and an amber pip mark the part you will not get, and the readout says how far short. A wireframe hull at the destination shows the attitude you end the turn in. Commanding a heading switches to Slide, because Move points the nose wherever thrust is needed and cannot honour a facing order.
Click or tap own ship: select · same on an enemy: target
A/D rotate 15° · F face target · Enter end turn
RMB drag orbits · middle drag pans · wheel zooms · timeline slider scrubs the ghost estimators along every path.
Enemy paths are estimates (momentum) - real orders stay hidden until resolution, lockstep-style.

The row under the map works everywhere: ⟳ / ✥ swaps what a drag outside the ring does between swinging the camera and sliding it, ⊕ snaps the camera onto the selected ship, and the five on the right mirror E/Q/A/D/F. They grey out when no ship is selected.

On touch: one finger inside the ring drags the destination, one finger outside it flies the camera, and two fingers pinch to zoom and slide the view. Tap a sheet's tab again to close it, or tap the map.

The envelope is probed from the sim, not drawn from a formula: every cell goes through the same planner the turn resolver uses. Boost and Stop therefore show one committed point instead of a volume, and when the movement model changes the shape changes with it.
