Drag in space to place the destination. It lands on the working altitude, which is the same plane the envelope is sliced at, so the two are one idea rather than a hidden number.
Q/E raise and lower that plane by 5. Watch the slice shrink as you climb away from the hull: that is the spherical slice, and it is what the altitude costs you in lateral room.
Drag the white pip on the ring at the destination, or shift-drag, to swing the heading.
Click or tap own ship: select · same on an enemy: target
A/D rotate 15° · F face target · Enter end turn
RMB drag orbits · wheel zooms · timeline slider scrubs the ghost estimators along every path.
Enemy paths are estimates (momentum) - real orders stay hidden until resolution, lockstep-style.

On touch: one finger drags the destination, two fingers orbit and pinch to zoom, and the round pad at the right edge covers the keys a phone does not have. Tap a sheet's tab again to close it, or tap the map.

The envelope is probed from the sim, not drawn from a formula: every cell goes through the same planner the turn resolver uses. Boost and Stop therefore show one committed point instead of a volume, and when the movement model changes the shape changes with it.
