Exterior

Layers

Plate is authored one face at a time. Layers set what a face absorbs, and a gun with penetration is scored against fewer of them, so a belt that stops a beam is porous to a shell. The frame under it is not editable in either mode.

Paint

Armour only, and all eight of a faction's swatches are on the hull at once: plating panels, a dark underside, a dorsal spine, a stripe along the waist, a nose flash and a transom band. Picking a swatch sets the primary; the other seven roles hold, so the scheme survives a re-tint. Everything else on the ship is coloured by what it does, so a drive is orange and a gun is red whoever built them. Cosmetic either way: never hashed, never sent to the core, so two players who painted the same hull differently cannot read as a desync.
