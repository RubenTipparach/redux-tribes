INTRO (this section is more for the reader; maybe I'll put this in as black screen text to briefly explain the setting)
In the distant future, humans abandoned Earth in a great exile after the ecosystem collapsed. Without realizing it, they had committed a grave galactic offense—breaking one of the three laws imposed by the Galactic Council upon every sentient race.

Law 1: A sentient race or group of people shall not eradicate another.
Law 2: The tenure of a sentient race or group shall not cause the destruction or collapse of a planet's biosphere.
Law 3: No method of travel shall meet or exceed the speed of light relative to an observer.

The violation of any of these laws will result in destruction of that race or group of sentient beings.

Thanks for reading so far! Yes, I'm looking for general improvements on how to be a better Sci-Fi writer. Its a skill I've neglected for a while. So I'll give you a high level view of where I want this story to go.

**Tutorial Section** (currently this is what the opening dialog is for, I came up with a script annotation with the AI to parse these lines into the game, hence the :: and ->)
- This section introduces the main character, crews, various leaders and key figures in the conflict.
- Sets the general tone for the story, this is closer to hard sci-fi with some grander elements to discuss the various Kardashev scale of civilizations, why they do what they do, and the goals to expand. We also live in a dark forest universe (I'm taking lots of inspiration from 3 body problem universe, but not as bleak), meaning aliens are like animals or tribes in a dark forest. They attack one another for fear of being attacked themselves.
- It's clear that the human colonies aren't surviving very well without a decent planet to settle on that can sustain mass farming and resources required for energy production. Their desperation leads to the current setting of the plot where tensions with a neighboring faction has grown to its boiling point, and that the Terran Empire, lead by a mad emperor is on the verge of a large scale invasion of the Karisen home world. Clearly, the Karisens in order to protect themselves, have made plans to frame the Terrans by causing their home world to be destroyed in the invasion, and thus a conspiracy within the Terran Naval Intelligence and Karisens who are against the idea has appointed Captain Geril as the lead role in stopping the emperor.

- The tutorial missions, as a fun idea I'm currently working on various writing for introduces each key officer on the bridge as they walk you through the various ship systems. Still cleaning up some dialog there, will probably be implementing that as a goal for the 2D con showcase.

**The mid Section**
The New World becomes a front to explore the rest of the nebula. It turns out the humand and the Karisens are not the only ones inhabbiting it. There are many other factions, some that live there to tread with other factions that lie deeper and have more exotic technologies. Cultists, and galactic council rebels also inhabit the nebula, as well as a faction that manufactures advanced weapons that they believe can be used against the council. All of these factions operate in a kind of grey area, they don't quite trust one another and might be hostile.

**The End Game**
- The Galactic Council represents a group of aliens who have become so far technologically advanced, that they believe that the dark forest hypothesis can become something of a zoo. They can tame the lesser alien factions to "behave" with one another and ascend to become the next generation of the council. The council is working on constant research to ascend to a higher dimension and leave this universe, it is unclear if this goal is attainable before the heat death.
- In their persuit, and their massive economic structures that lie within the core regions of the galaxy, they've needed to dip into various worlds to take resources that they require. In doing so they exploited many worlds and lesser cultures under the veil of  "humane" migrations. This also included the attack on Earth carried out by a proxy race to cause humans to abandone the planet and solar system. Many within the nebula who are in hiding, know of the various atrocities commited by the council and seek top bring it down and cause a revolution for a different kind of galactic rule.
