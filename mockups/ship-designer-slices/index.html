Tap an empty cell to place the selected kind. Tap a filled cell to replace it. Erase is its own palette chip, so no tap ever means two things. Drag across the grid to paint a run.

The 3D view takes orbit only and never a placement, which is the whole point of the variant: a flat cell cannot be picked by accident the way a cube behind another cube can.

The only arithmetic outside askTheCore is framing: how many pixels the grid gets, where a cube sits in the scene, how tall a bar in the slice rail is drawn. Nothing that decides an outcome.
