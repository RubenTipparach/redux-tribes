Starship silhouettes

The bolt on pass (decorFor in web/src/app/design.ts) now speaks the language every starship on television speaks: warp nacelles on pylons, wings, fins, a deflector lit in the bow. Each navy says it its own way and keeps the habit it had before. Every rung of a navy gets the same dress, because the geometry is authored in fractions of the waist and sited by the frame's own gun rings. All of it is armour and costs armour's mass; the class table was re-measured from the fleet.

What is being decided: whether these silhouettes are the fleet's look. Approved, they ship as they are here; rejected, the pass goes back to strakes, rails and blisters and the table is re-measured. The photographs are the yard's own, taken by tools/fleet_shots.mjs style drives of the real screen; nothing is mocked.
