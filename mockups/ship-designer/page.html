Undo is on canvas, next to the slice steppers, not in here. A control that lives only in a panel is a control nothing on screen says exists.

Tap an empty cell to place the selected kind. Tap a filled cell of the same kind to take it back, so correcting one mistap costs one tap. Erase and Probe are their own chips, so no tap ever means two things. Drag across the grid to paint a run; the stroke is walked between samples, so a fast flick paints every cell on the path rather than the two ends.

The 3D view takes orbit only and never a placement, which is the whole point of the variant: a flat cell cannot be picked by accident the way a cube behind another cube can.

The only arithmetic outside askTheCore is framing: how many pixels the grid gets, where a cube sits in the scene, how tall a bar in the slice rail is drawn. Nothing that decides an outcome.

every number below comes from the core

One call, askTheCore(IN, OUT), standing in for ft_read_design(). Two flat Float32Arrays cross, nothing else: no strings, no objects, no colours. Mass, hull, thrust, legality, the module table, the class table: the client does not work any of them out, it prints what came back and owns the words.
