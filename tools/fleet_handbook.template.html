The lineup

Plan view, one scale, common baseline

A shipyard frames every hull to fill its view, so a corvette and a heavy cruiser photograph the same size and no screenshot can answer is it actually bigger. These are drawn from the station profiles to a single scale. The voxel is one size for the whole game, so a bigger ship is more cells rather than larger ones, and the ladder you see here is the lattice ratio and nothing else.

What a rung buys

Hull points, up each navy's ladder

A course of armour is one cell and a cell is the same size everywhere, so a cruiser plated to a frigate's six courses would wear a frigate's thickness over twice the ship. Courses are cut to the lattice instead: plate volume goes as skin area times thickness, so hull goes as the cube of length. That is the shape of these bars, and it is why a heavy cruiser does not die to another heavy cruiser in one turn.
